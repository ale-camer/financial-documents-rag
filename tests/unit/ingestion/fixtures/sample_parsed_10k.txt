SAMPLE FINANCIAL CORP.

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Sample Financial Corp. is an enterprise technology company providing cloud infrastructure,
analytics, and AI-powered workflow automation software for financial institutions globally.
The company was founded in 2018 and has expanded its operations to over 25 countries.

Our products include CorePlatform, DataStream Engine, and ComplianceSuite. We serve over
1,500 enterprise customers, primarily Tier-1 banks, investment managers, and fintechs.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Our business, financial condition, and results of operations could be materially and adversely
affected by the following risks:

Macroeconomic Volatility: Global economic downturns, inflation, and changing interest rates
may cause our clients to reduce or defer spending on financial software technologies.

Cybersecurity and Data Privacy: We process sensitive financial data. Security breaches,
ransomware attacks, or unauthorized data access could harm our reputation and operations.

ITEM 2. PROPERTIES

Our corporate headquarters is located in San Francisco, California, consisting of approximately

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Overview
Total revenue for fiscal year 2023 was $450.5 million, representing an increase of 22% compared
to $369.2 million in fiscal year 2022. Subscription revenue accounted for 88% of total revenue.

Operating Results
Gross margin improved to 76% from 73% in the prior year, driven by economies of scale in our cloud
infrastructure and higher customer net retention rates of 118%.

Liquidity and Capital Resources
As of December 31, 2023, we had cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments of $185.0 million.
We believe our existing cash balances will be sufficient to meet working capital requirements for at
least the next 12 months.

ITEM 7A. QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE DISCLOSURES ABOUT MARKET RISK

Interest Rate Risk: We hold a portfolio of cash equivalents and short-term marketable securities. A
hypothetical 100 basis point increase in interest rates would not have a material impact on earnings.

Foreign Exchange Risk: We conduct business internationally and are exposed to foreign currency
fluctuations, primarily the Euro, British Pound, and Japanese Yen.
